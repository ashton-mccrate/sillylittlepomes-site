privacy

how Little While handles your data.

Little While collects nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics, and no tracking of any kind — the app makes no network connections at all. It has nothing to send, and we never see how you use it.

The little whiles you pick and your handful of settings are saved only on your device, so they're waiting the next time you open the app. They stay on your phone, and they leave with it when you delete the app.

Little While is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13.
